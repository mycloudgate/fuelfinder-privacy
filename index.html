Privacy Policy

Fuel Finder — by MyCloudGate

Last updated: 23 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Fuel Finder ("the App", "we", "us") accesses, how it is used, and what your choices are. Fuel Finder is developed by MyCloudGate, an independent Android developer based in Spain. This policy applies to both the free version (Fuel Finder, contains ads) and the paid version (Fuel Finder Pro, no ads) of the app.

1. What data the App accesses

Location. If you grant permission, the App uses your device's location to center the map near you and to show fuel stations in your area. Your location is used only on your device to determine what to display on the map — it is not sent to, or stored on, any server operated by MyCloudGate. You can deny or revoke this permission at any time in your device's Settings; the App will still work, showing a default area instead of your current location.

Fuel price data. The App fetches publicly available fuel station and price information from MyCloudGate's own server, which in turn sources this data from Spain's Ministry for the Ecological Transition (official government open data on fuel prices). This request does not include your location, device identifiers, or any personal information — it simply retrieves the full public dataset, which the App then filters and displays locally.

No account, no personal data collection by us. The App does not require you to create an account, log in, or provide any personal information (name, email, phone number, etc.) to MyCloudGate. We do not operate any user database.

2. Third-party services

The App relies on a small number of third-party services to function. Each is governed by its own privacy policy, linked below:

  • Google Maps SDK for Android and Google Places API — used to display the map and to let you search for a location. Google's handling of data through these services is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google AdMob Free version only — the free version of the App shows banner advertisements via Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as your device's advertising ID) and use them to show ads, including personalized ads based on your interests, subject to your device's ad settings. You can opt out of personalized advertising, or reset your advertising ID, in your device's Settings (Google Settings → Ads). See the Google Ads Privacy FAQ for details. Fuel Finder Pro does not include AdMob or any advertising SDK.

3. Data retention

Since MyCloudGate does not collect or store personal data through the App, there is no personal data retention period to disclose. Fuel price data cached on your device is stored locally only, for the App's own offline/quick-access functionality, and can be cleared at any time by clearing the App's storage in your device's Settings, or by uninstalling the App.

4. Children's privacy

Fuel Finder is not directed at children under 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your region) and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Your choices

  • Location permission can be granted, denied, or revoked at any time via your device's App Settings.
  • Ad personalization (free version) can be adjusted or opted out of via your device's Google Settings → Ads.
  • You may uninstall the App at any time, which removes all locally cached data.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example to reflect changes in the App's functionality or in applicable law. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
